fill → trade reconstruction prototype · throwaway

Question. Does "a Trade is one position round-trip on one symbol and one side, scale-ins and partial exits folded into weighted-average entry/exit" survive contact with realistic Binance USDⓈ-M fills? The model under test is a journal state { fills, seeds, trades, cursors } and one action, sync, which takes an exchange snapshot (new fills from userTrades, open positions from positionRisk, conditional orders from openAlgoOrders, a BNBUSDT 1-minute price table) and returns the next journal state. Trades are rebuilt from stored fills on every sync; nothing is accumulated on the trade row itself. Watch for: weighted averages, the flip split, hedge mode, positions that began before the 30-day window, BNB fees, the SL/TP snapshot freezing at close, and re-syncing the same data twice.

Time shown as UTC+8. Window start = 30 days before "now". Numbers are fixtures, not real market data.
